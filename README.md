# My Weird Magic Academy Life

Welcome to the least normal magic university ever created.

My Weird Magic Academy Life is a magic academy life simulator game developed with Unity.  
Players spend three years at a strange magical academy where attending class is optional, surviving chaos is not, and every student seems slightly insane.

Study magic, build friendships, work part-time jobs, explore campus mysteries, and somehow graduate without destroying reality.

---

## Gameplay Concept

Players will live through three years at a magical university.

During their time at the academy, they can:
- Attend classes
- Improve magic skills
- Make friends
- Earn money
- Explore campus locations

Every decision may affect the final ending.
